    How to use

    • Find a bhajan: type a few letters of the name, its number, or a raga in the search box. Spelling doesn't need to be exact. Tap a deity (Ganesha, Guru…) to see only those.
    • Play: tap ▶ next to a bhajan. The player at the bottom has pause, previous, next and a slider to move within the song.
    • Repeat: tap Repeat on the player to play the same bhajan again and again. Tap it again to stop.
    • Slower: tap 1× to slow the song to 0.9× or 0.75× for practice.
    • Details: tap a bhajan's name to see the photo, pitch for gents and ladies, raga, beat and lyrics.
    • Pitch: "2 Madhyam" or "5 Pancham" is the harmonium setting; "Sa = G" is the note for Sa. "Sung at" shows the pitch of the recording.
    • Favourites: open a bhajan and tap Add to favourites. Find them under the Favourites tab.
    • Without internet: a song played once with internet stays on your phone. A green dot on ▶ means it is saved. To save everything, use Save all songs at the bottom of the list (best on Wi‑Fi).
    • Share: open a bhajan and tap Share to send its link on WhatsApp.
    • Install on your phone: Android: tap Install app, or ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    • Updates: new bhajans appear by themselves when you open the app with internet. If something looks old, close and reopen the app with internet. The app version is shown at the bottom of the list.
    • Grey ▶: the recording for that bhajan has not been added yet.
